Inji Certify — Issuer Demo

Real OpenID4VCI issuance against a local Certify instance and MOSIP Collab's mock eSignet. Issuer piece only — not the team's playground UI.

  1. (Optional) Edit the identity on the left and save — this restarts Certify, so it takes about a minute.
  2. Pick a credential format on the right.
  3. Click "Issue credential" and watch the protocol steps run.
  4. Click any step to see its raw request/response. The result at the bottom shows the decoded credential, with the raw form behind a toggle.

Inji Verify — Present & Verify Demo

A holder wallet presents a credential issued by Inji Certify to a local Inji Verify over OpenID4VP. The playground runs its own checks next to Inji Verify's and flags where they disagree. Verifier piece only — not the team's playground UI.

  1. Receive a credential into the wallet on the left (from Certify, through Chirayu's issuance flow).
  2. Pick the format and a scenario. "Honest" should be accepted; the others should be rejected.
  3. Click "Present with built-in wallet" and watch verifier and wallet talk. Or show a QR code to a phone wallet.
  4. The result compares what Inji Verify said with what it should have said, and lists the checks on both sides.
